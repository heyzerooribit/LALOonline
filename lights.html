Leave a light on

Every light is a promise to stay. Tap a box to leave a light on. Your lights stay lit on this device.

Press and hold the title for four seconds to turn all the lights off and start over.

Leave a Light On is a student public advocacy project created for a communication course. It offers general awareness information and is not a substitute for professional care. Resources and statistics are drawn from the CDC, NIMH, and the 988 Lifeline; verify current details on their official sites.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988.

Use it like an app.

On iPhone or iPad: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android or a computer: open your browser menu and choose Install app.
