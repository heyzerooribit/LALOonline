Notice when a light goes out

Signs are not always dramatic. Look for changes from a person’s usual self, especially new, sudden, or growing ones.

What they say

  • Talking about wanting to die or having no reason to live
  • Feeling trapped or like a burden to others
  • Unbearable pain, emotional or physical
  • Saying goodbye in unusual ways

What they do

  • Pulling away from friends, class, or activities
  • Giving away things that matter to them
  • Big changes in sleep or eating
  • Increased alcohol or drug use
  • Searching for ways to end their life

How they seem

  • Hopelessness or deep despair
  • Rage, agitation, or reckless behavior
  • Sudden calm after a long low period
  • Extreme mood swings

Risk is higher after a loss, a breakup, a failure, bullying, a major life change, or when someone feels alone in a new place. If you see these signs, act, even if you’re not sure.
