Get help now

If you’re struggling, you deserve support. These services are free, confidential, and open all day, every day.

988 Suicide & Crisis LifelineCall or text 988Chat online at 988lifeline.org. Veterans can press 1. Spanish is available.
Crisis Text LineText HOME to 741741Free support by text with a trained crisis counselor.
The Trevor ProjectText START to 678-678Support for LGBTQ+ young people. Call 1-866-488-7386 or visit thetrevorproject.org.
On campusCounseling centerMost colleges offer free or low-cost counseling. Search your school’s website for “counseling services.”
Immediate dangerCall 911Or go to the nearest emergency room. Stay with the person until help arrives.
Outside the USfindahelpline.comFind a free helpline in your country.
