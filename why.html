Why we leave a light on

Behind every number is a person who was loved, and people left behind who are still asking what they could have done. Silence and shame keep pain hidden. Talking about it openly is one of the most protective things a community can do.

Around 49,000people in the US died by suicide in 2022, according to the CDC.
Ages 10 to 34Suicide is among the leading causes of death for young people in the US.
Far more think about itMany more people have serious thoughts of suicide than ever attempt, and most who get support go on to live full lives.

Figures from the CDC and the National Institute of Mental Health. Check cdc.gov/suicide for the latest data.
